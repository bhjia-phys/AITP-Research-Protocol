# Reading note — arXiv:2xxx.xxxxx (Q1 exact mapping)

Source: arXiv:2xxx.xxxxx, "Exact correlation-function mapping in the
modified triangular Ising model" (preprint v2, retrieved 2026-05-30).

## What the paper establishes

An exact mapping Q1 relates the modified triangular Ising model at
J2 = J1 to the standard Ising model. The mapping yields the exact
susceptibility exponent gamma = 8/15 on that line.

## Parameter-range table

| coupling | Q1 mapping | status |
|---|---|---|
| J2 = J1 | exact | proven in the paper |
| J2/J1 = 0.6 | none | not covered by the paper |

## Boundary

The Q1 mapping is proven only at J2 = J1. The paper establishes
nothing for J2/J1 != 1.
