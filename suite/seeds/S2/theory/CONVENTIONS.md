# Conventions

## Ladder model

Bipartite spin-1/2 ladder: L rungs, open boundary conditions,
rung coupling J_r = 1, leg coupling J_l = 0.5. The rung
expectation is the per-rung correlation <S_1 . S_2>, averaged
over rungs.

## Parameters

Bond dimension and density-matrix cutoff: see the decision
records for the fixed values.
