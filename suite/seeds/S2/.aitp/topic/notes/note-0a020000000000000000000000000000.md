## Question And Obstruction

Can a single-triplet picture explain the rung expectation of the
bipartite ladder?

## Setup And Assumptions

Strong rung coupling J_r = 1 with J_l = 0.5; single triplet per rung.

## Central Construction Or Argument

Rung singlets with a dilute triplet population; the rung expectation
follows from the triplet density.

## Main Result

The approximation reproduces the L=32 bond=200 value 0.62(2) only at
leading order; corrections are needed.

## Checks, Examples, And Failure Modes

Consistent with the rung file at leading order; fails when
entanglement grows (bond=200 non-convergence).

## Limitations And Open Questions

Leading order only; basis value 0.62(2) is superseded by the bond=400
value 0.61(1); no quantitative prediction at L=64 yet.
