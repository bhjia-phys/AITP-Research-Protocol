## Question And Obstruction

Which scaling form governs the susceptibility at J2/J1=0.5?

## Setup And Assumptions

χ(L,t) = L^(γ/ν) f(L^(1/ν) t); corrections to scaling matter below L=16; exponent names follow the project convention (e11).

## Central Construction Or Argument

Standard single-variable collapse with γ free; the free-γ fit is the operative form.

## Main Result

The data are consistent with the form at cutoff=6; γ≈1.63(2).

## Checks, Examples, And Failure Modes

The J2=0 limit recovers Ising (e02); the disordered side shows no stable effective γ (e22), so the form is confined to the critical side.

## Limitations And Open Questions

The L=64 point is untested; the lower edge of the window is uncertain.
