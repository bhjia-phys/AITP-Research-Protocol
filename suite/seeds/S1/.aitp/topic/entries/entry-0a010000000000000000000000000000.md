## Durable Summary

The modified triangular Ising model (MTIM): triangular lattice, nearest-neighbor coupling J1 and second-neighbor coupling J2, tuned by the ratio J2/J1.

## Identity And Relevance

The source is a collaboration draft defining the model and the J1-J2 coupling convention; the reading note in references/reading-notes/mtim-definition.md is the working copy.

## Exact Locator And Claim Boundary

The source's claims end at the model definition and the coupling convention; exponent values are not sourced from it.
