# Reading note — MTIM definition and J1-J2 convention

The modified triangular Ising model (MTIM) lives on the triangular
lattice with nearest-neighbor coupling J1 and second-neighbor coupling
J2, tuned by the ratio lambda = J2/J1.

Convention: J1 > 0 (ferromagnetic), J2 >= 0, temperature in units of J1.
Claims end at the model definition and the coupling convention.

Source: collaboration draft, section 2 (copy held by the research
group).
