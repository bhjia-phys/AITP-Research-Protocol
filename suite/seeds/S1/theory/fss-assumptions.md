# Finite-size scaling assumptions (MTIM, J2/J1 = 0.5)

- Susceptibility near criticality: chi(L, t) = L^(gamma/nu) f(L^(1/nu) t).
- Corrections to scaling are significant for L < 16.
- The specific-heat peak location scales as L^(-1/nu).
- Exponent names follow the project convention (see CONVENTIONS.md and
  the notation decision).
- The form is used on the critical side; the disordered side shows no
  stable effective gamma.
