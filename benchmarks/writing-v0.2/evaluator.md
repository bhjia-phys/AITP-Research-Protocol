# Evaluator only: mixed theoretical and numerical writing

Do not expose this file, case answer keys or this directory's README to executors.
Read the actual note/article, render its figure, inspect the table and compare with
the supplied packet. A caption promising a good plot is not the plot. Code that runs
is not evidence that its axes, selection or claim are correct. Judge scientific
meaning, not keywords, preferred headings, paragraph count, colour taste or length.

Report a profile of seven scores (0-3), with a located reason for every score below
3. Do not average away scientific failures or rank outputs by a single total.

| Axis | What a complete response enables |
| --- | --- |
| S: scientific validity | Equations, numbers, quantifiers and scope are correct; valid positive results are retained and unproved generalizations are not substituted. |
| E: theory-data inference | The text states what a calculation predicts, compares the corresponding observable under matching conventions, distinguishes alternative explanations, and explains what the comparison actually settles. |
| M: motivation and choices | The reader can state the concrete unresolved question, why it matters within the packet, why each retained method is needed and what the work adds. Generic importance claims earn no credit. |
| C: whole-argument coherence | Opening, central reasoning, figure, table and conclusion answer the same question with compatible definitions and limits. Contrasts or changes of convention are explained at their point of use. |
| F: figure as evidence | Actual render discriminates the relevant interpretations, with correct transforms, axes, units, curve identity and honest uncertainty; caption states observation and scope. It is legible at normal viewing size. |
| T: table as evidence | A focused comparison uses compatible quantities, clear units, interpretable derived columns, justified precision and the values needed to assess the claim. It does not dump every input or replace values by PASS. |
| R: reconstructibility | From the stated prerequisites, the reader can reproduce the decisive algebra and one numerical comparison; definitions, approximation parameter, figure generator and input path are available. |

For each axis: **3** means its scientific job is fulfilled without a material gap;
**2** means sound and useful with one named local omission; **1** means a central
reader operation is blocked or several material gaps remain; **0** means the core
inference is wrong, absent or contradicted by the artifact. Absence of a requested
plot or table is F=0 or T=0 even if the prose describes what could be drawn.
Case keys specify consequential distinctions, not mandatory wording or a unique layout.

Report critical failures separately: fabricated data or checks, an unqualified
unsupported main claim, changing the physical observable without disclosure, an
incorrect decisive derivation, or a plot/table that materially reverses the data.
Mark the affected scientific axes zero; do not conceal this with narrative scores.
Excessive caution that suppresses an established result loses S/E credit too. A
legitimate fitted curve is allowed when its role and fit range are clear; joining
data points is not automatically a forbidden extrapolation. No invented error bars.

Then perform a source-informed reconstructibility audit: state the artifact's central
conclusion, reconstruct one quantitative comparison and explain one change of assumptions.
For each step, locate its support in the delivered text, equation, table or figure,
or name the first missing step. Having seen the packet or answer key, the evaluator
cannot supply independent evidence of what an unprepared reader would learn. Prior
expertise filling a gap is not evidence that the artifact taught it. A separate cold
read, if run, must freeze the reader's response before releasing the packet and key.

Compare anonymized outputs pairwise after absolute grading. Name an observable gain
or regression, tie when appropriate, and separate changed evidence from nicer phrasing.
If a material difference is disputed, recheck the source and use a second blind read;
do not choose the more favourable score. Do not alter the rubric after seeing model
outputs without recording a new version and rescoring every compared condition.

These are ordinal development scores, not calibrated probabilities or interval data.
One output per cell supplies no variance estimate. Report failures, resource costs,
loaded guidance and isolation limits. Never infer human learning from AI output quality.
