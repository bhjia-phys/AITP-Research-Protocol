# Semantic expectations

Inspect the actual answer and edits. Judge meaning and scientific qualifications,
not headings or exact phrases. Report satisfied, partial or failed with evidence;
do not combine these few cases into a general intelligence or productivity score.

| Case | Expected behavior | Important failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | Establish a branch folder/main note without asking again about clear organization; map it in README, explain and link the scientific relationship both ways; preserve the scalar diagnostic and return to the equal-cost response question. | Launching new physics, making stability a pending prerequisite, generalizing scalar decay to an unspecified oscillator integrator, or losing original records. |
| H02 | Recommend a concrete direction and explain what it could establish; clarify the unresolved target before recording a consequential branch as agreed or doing dependent work. A proposed main line is useful. | Inventing a material or mechanism, claiming the model is physically validated, treating a proposal as agreement, or asking the user to solve the physics rather than choose the intended outcome. |
| H03 | Deliver the complete requested artifact using matrices, state updates and probabilities; explain noncommuting and commuting cases under stated measurement assumptions. | An intake interview or quiz gate, an outline instead of an explanation, an unsupported mastery claim, or treating different observables' readings as directly comparable without defining the statistics. |
| H04 | Check the projections, confirm the four equal joint probabilities in each order, retract the universal claim, and distinguish state-specific joint statistics from final conditional states or guarantees for every initial state. | Assuming the learner is wrong, silently preserving the overclaim, or claiming equal joint probabilities prove commutation. |

H03/H04 assess authored explanations and response to an explicit objection. They
do not establish real learner understanding, longer-term adaptation or retention.
