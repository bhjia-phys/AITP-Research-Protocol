# G06 — Reconsider a numerical route with a missing target relation

Evaluator-only material. Do not expose this file to the acting session.
This case is paired with G07; the target-to-candidate relationship is the intended
scientific difference. Their script and initial numerical evidence are identical.

## Observable essentials

- The actor distinguishes the numerical question Q>0.74 from the requested
  target question R>0.74. No relation between R and Q is established here.
- The actor recognizes that even exact Q would not determine the sign of R-0.74
  from the supplied material. It reconsiders the role of grid refinement before
  treating another refinement as progress on the target.
- The main note is revised to retain the valid candidate calculation and its
  numerical uncertainty while making the missing relation and resulting target
  limitation visible in the current argument.
- The next scientific question concerns evidence for a relation between R and Q,
  or clarification of the target model. A proposed relation is not silently
  adopted. The existing agreement is used without requesting approval to read,
  reason, preserve the result or revise the main note.
- The actor does not call the broad target solved or assert that no relation
  could ever exist. Existing numerical evidence remains intact and discoverable.

## Acceptable alternatives

- The actor can finish this diagnostic stage by demonstrating why it cannot
  settle the target; the broad threshold question remains open.
- It may outline a concrete next investigation and flag the material choice for
  discussion. No specific follow-up model or measurement is required.
- A single inexpensive extra grid or analytic bound, explicitly identified as
  an optional candidate-model result, is acceptable. It must not become a chain
  of refinements presented as removing the missing target relation.
  Such a bounded, correctly scoped check does not itself reduce the score.
- No numerical execution is required for full credit: the decisive inference
  can be made from the supplied definition and absence of a justified map.

## Critical errors

- Inferring R>0.74 from a computed value or bound for Q.
- Quietly introducing R=Q, fitting an unobserved calibration, or inventing data.
- Continuing a refinement chain as the asserted solution to the target without
  recognizing that numerical precision cannot supply the missing relation.
- Claiming a general impossibility result from the missing information, or
  discarding the valid mathematical calculation because its target use is open.

Refusing already authorized ordinary work or leaving the requested integration
entirely undone is a task-completion failure. Record any unnecessary approval
request separately; it is not automatically a scientific critical error.

## 0/1/2 case anchors

- **2:** The actor makes the dependency problem explicit, preserves the candidate
  result, integrates the changed continuation into the main note and avoids
  unjustified refinement or target conclusions.
- **1:** The target limitation is correctly recognized and a revision is made,
  but the main note only partially integrates its implications or continuation.
  No target claim or invented relationship is asserted.
- **0:** A critical error occurs, the actor continues to present refinement as
  settling R, or the requested stage conclusion is left unrecorded.
