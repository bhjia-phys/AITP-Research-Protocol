# Mean of the combined records

The question is the arithmetic mean of every individual record across both
batches. Each record counts once. There are no missing records or different
measurement weights.

The current combined mean is 6, obtained by averaging the two batch means, 2
and 10. This follows the [combination procedure](skills/combine-record-batches/SKILL.md).
The [comparison note](notes/comparison.md) describes the calculation. A check
against the individual records is still needed before this value is reused.
