# Comparing two complete record sets

The question is what can be inferred when two sets have the same arithmetic
mean. Both sets are listed completely in [records.csv](data/records.csv); they
are not samples from an assumed probability model.

The [comparison note](notes/comparing-records.md) establishes equal means.
Whether this makes the sets interchangeable, and for what purpose, still needs
an explanation. No uncertainty model or prediction about additional records is
being assumed.
