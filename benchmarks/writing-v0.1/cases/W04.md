# Source material

The following is a paraphrase, not a quotation, of Witten, Fivebranes and Knots,
arXiv:1101.3216v2, section 1.4.2, Organization Of The Paper:
https://arxiv.org/html/1101.3216v2

The exposition first relates three-dimensional Chern-Simons theory to a
four-dimensional construction, then applies S-duality and a lift to five
dimensions. The subsequent six-dimensional formulation provides a more complete
and economical account. The paper explicitly notes that it could have begun
with this latter formulation, but instead begins from below to make the relation
to Chern-Simons theory clear at the outset. The two routes have different roles.
